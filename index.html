November 19th marks the anniversary of Tokyo Alley, and in 2026, we're celebrating 10 years!

A whole decade of bringing people together, celebrating the things we love, and creating unforgettable memories. And what better way to celebrate than with a birthday party?

Help Touko plan the perfect birthday celebration for Sensei, and by the end you'll discover which Australian convention mascot best matches your personality!

About Tokyo Alley

Tokyo Alley Perth is Western Australia's longest-running anime and pop-culture convention. As a volunteer-run,
non-profit organisation, we're passionate about cultivating creativity within our community and beyond.

Thank you for being a part of our journey!
